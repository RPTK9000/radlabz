Twisted TVC - 30”

Passport office.

We open to a camera flash and a freeze frame of our protagonist failing his passport photo yet again.

SFX: We hear the camera flash

Photographer: Mr Schmidt !
Mr Schmidt adjusts himself and prepares to try again.

Photographer: Ok, not a problem let's try again Mr. Schmidt straight into the camera...

SFX: We hear the hum of a halogen light globe that occasionally flickers in the background.
SFX: We hear the camera flash.

At that moment, we see the frame freeze on Mr Schmidt and fade to black.

Photographer: Mr. Schmidt!
Mr Schmidt adjusts himself, no sweaty and visibly stressed, this time taking a deep breath, before getting
ready to try again.
SFX: We hear the camera flash

At that moment, we see the frame freeze on Mr. Schmidt

Photographer: Ok Mr. Schmidt ... (defeated)
We see Mr Schmidt sweat as he composes himself gets ready to try again.

SFX: We hear the the Photographer sigh and pack up their things. Footsteps quietly fade away as we hear
the click of a few light switches before a door shuts.
The hum of the light bulb has stopped as the room is now pitch black.

We’re just able to make out Mr Schmidt. He’s still sitting there. Motionless.

SFX: We hear nothing
We see the Bonds logo appear on screen. 

V/O: Don’t get it twisted
